OpenRoot

Offline-first shelter, heat, and compute — stood up on a phone and a used PC.

η = useful_joules / human_joules

Compute

  • A local 7B coder runs on an OptiPlex; the phone is the client over an SSH tunnel. No cloud inference.
  • Every pathway is sealed: plan - execute - verify - seal into an append-only hash-linked ledger.
  • Seals verify in microseconds. Work is only recomputed on failure.

Heat

  • AeroCement open-cell panels — drill-and-bucket buildable.
  • Stirling on real delta-T. Carnot-bounded, flux-limited. No free-energy claims.
  • Black Locust rocket mass heater + earth battery. Capture heat once, route it, store it.

Integrity

  • Gate before commit: syntax checks, compile checks, first-principles table drift checks.
  • Sealed receipts with every release: GATE_RECEIPT, BUILD_STATUS, append-only WORK_LOG.
  • Two-node mesh: payloads travel by Syncthing; truth travels by hash-linked ledger.
